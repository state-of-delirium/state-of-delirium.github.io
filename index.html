About This Site

This website is a weird brainchild of Pratham and Vishnu, a place to pen down our thoughts and ideas. It's still under construction and we hope to add much more IndieWeb-y components.

Each post is also tagged with a metric that reflects our state of mind while writing it: Delirious, Nebulous, and Lucid. In more detail,

  • Delirious — barely conscious chaotic ramblings
  • Nebulous — coherent but hazy and abstract
  • Lucid — work you better feel smarter by reading
