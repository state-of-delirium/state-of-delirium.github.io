### What's That Wi-Fi Looking Symbol I Clicked On?
It's an <abbr>RSS</abbr> (Really Simple Syndication) feed.
Like how you subscribe to a channel on YouTube, whenever there is a new post on the website, you will be notified on your feed reader.

### I'm Still Interested
Here's a cookie 🍪<br>
Now, feed readers are applications where you view the feeds you have subscribed to. You can aggregate the content of all of your favorite sites in a single place, without having to go check each and every one of them all the time.

Plenty of websites have feeds, you simply might've not noticed them. For example, basically every single news site has a feed--usually many feeds, each for a category!

### Which Reader Do I Get?
_"I wanna subscribe to my fav sites"_
- [Fluent](https://hyliu.me/fluent-reader/) --  all desktops
- [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/) -- Mac/iOS
- [Feeder](https://github.com/spacecowboy/Feeder) -- Android

_"I wanna discover new feeds"_
- [Feedly](https://feedly.com/news-reader) -- proprietary, freemium, simple
- [NewsBlur](https://www.newsblur.com/) -- open source, freemium, extra features
